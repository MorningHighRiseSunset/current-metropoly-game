How to Play Craps

Basic Rules

Come-Out Roll: First roll of a new round. Roll 7 or 11 to win on Pass Line, 2, 3, or 12 (craps) to lose. Any other number becomes the "point."

Point Phase: Once a point is established, keep rolling until you either:

  • Roll the point again → Pass Line wins
  • Roll 7 → Pass Line loses (seven out)

Betting Options

  • Pass Line: Bet on shooter winning. Pays 1:1
  • Don't Pass: Bet on shooter losing. Pays 1:1
  • Come: Like Pass Line, but placed after point is established
  • Don't Come: Like Don't Pass, but placed after point is established
  • Field: Bet on 2, 3, 4, 9, 10, 11, or 12. Pays 1:1 (2:1 on 2 or 12)
  • Any Seven: Bet on next roll being 7. Pays 4:1
  • Any Craps: Bet on 2, 3, or 12. Pays 7:1
  • Hard Ways: Bet on specific doubles (4, 6, 8, 10). High payouts
  • Place Bets: Bet on specific numbers (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10)

How to Play

  1. Select a chip value ($5, $10, $25, $100)
  2. Click on betting areas to place bets
  3. Click "Roll Dice" to roll
  4. Watch your balance and win/loss tracking
